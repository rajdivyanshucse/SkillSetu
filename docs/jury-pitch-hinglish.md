# SkillSetu — Indian Presentation Pitch (Hinglish)
**Target Spoken Duration**: 2:30 – 3:00 Minutes | **Style**: Natural, Professional Indian Presentation Hinglish

---

### Verbatim Script:

Good morning esteemed jury members!

Agar aap aaj kisi traditional job portal ya career platform ko dekhein, toh wahan sab kuch sirf **resume keywords** aur search filters par chalta hai. Agar kisi candidate ke resume mein *Java*, *Spring Boot*, ya *Docker* likha hai, toh conventional ATS system usko instantly match kar deta hai.

Lekin asli challenge yeh hai: **Keyword likhne se yeh prove nahi hota ki candidate actually kya build kar sakta hai, target role ki realistic requirement kya hai, aur candidate ko aage kya seekhna chahiye.**

Isi problem ko solve karne ke liye humne banaya hai **SkillSetu**.

---

### Core Differentiator: Claimed Skill vs. Demonstrated Capability

SkillSetu keyword matching se aage badhkar **Evidence-Based Capability Intelligence** par kaam karta hai.

Hum **Claimed Skills** aur **Demonstrated Capabilities** ko strictly separate karte hain:
- Suppose candidate ne apne profile mein **Docker** mention kiya hai.
- Traditional system usko directly qualified maan leta hai.
- Lekin SkillSetu check karta hai ki kya candidate ke paas koi verified GitHub repository, pull request, ya project artifact hai?
- Agar zero evidence hai, toh system usko **Evidence Gap** mark karta hai.

---

### Adaptive Roadmap: Learn, Practice, Build, Validate, Re-evaluate

Yahan SkillSetu ka **Adaptive Roadmap** picture mein aata hai:
- Kyunki candidate ko Docker ka concept already pata hai, hum unko unnecessary beginner tutorials nahi dikhate.
- System seedha unhe **BUILD** stage par bhejta hai — jahan unhe ek real multi-tier containerized microservices project develop karna hota hai.
- Jaise hi candidate apna verified project submit karta hai, SkillSetu ka engine **Closed-Loop Re-evaluation** trigger karta hai:
  1. Docker ka state **Demonstrated** ban jaata hai.
  2. Roadmap automatically **Version 2** generate karta hai.
  3. Aur **Next Best Action** agle priority gap — jaise *Kubernetes* — par advance ho jaata hai.

---

### Enterprise Impact & Technical Stack

Organization level par, yehi capability model enterprise leaders ko **Multi-Horizon Scenario Planning** provide karta hai:
- Jisme companies decide kar sakti hain ki unhe talent **Hire** karna hai, internal team ko **Upskill** karna hai, ya **Redistribute** karna hai — bina kisi opaque employee surveillance ya biased ranking ke.

Under the hood, SkillSetu runs on **Node.js Express**, **Python FastAPI** with 384-dimensional multilingual embeddings, and **PostgreSQL with pgvector** backed by official international taxonomies — **O\*NET 29.1** and **ESCO v1.2.0**.

---

### Conclusion

To summarize:
**SkillSetu candidate ke claims, practical evidence, market direction, aur adaptive development ko ek transparent aur explainable loop mein connect karta hai.**

Thank you so much! Ab hum aapke questions ke liye ready hain.
