# SkillSetu — 3-Minute Competition Pitch
**Target Spoken Duration**: 2:45 – 3:15 Minutes | **Tone**: Confident, Professional, Technical, Conversational

---

### [0:00 – 0:30] The Opening Hook & Problem
Good morning, esteemed jury members.

If you search for a software engineer on conventional job portals, you will get hundreds of resumes claiming buzzwords like *Java*, *Spring Boot*, *Docker*, or *Machine Learning*. But a list of keywords tells us very little about what a candidate can actually build, what their target role realistically demands, or what exact steps they must take to bridge the gap.

Traditional recruitment platforms stop at keyword matching and opaque search filters. That creates a massive disconnect between what talent claims, what industry needs, and how professionals actually upskill.

---

### [0:30 – 1:05] The SkillSetu Solution & Capability Intelligence
That is why we built **SkillSetu**.

SkillSetu is a continuous capability-development and career intelligence platform. We move beyond keyword search by answering three fundamental questions:
1. What can this candidate actually *demonstrate* today?
2. What capabilities are becoming *more relevant* for their target career?
3. What is the *exact, adaptive path* to close their skill and evidence gaps?

We grounded our platform in official international labor taxonomies — **O\*NET 29.1** and **ESCO v1.2.0** — combined with multilingual semantic intelligence that understands English, Hindi, and Hinglish queries without false confusion.

---

### [1:05 – 1:50] The Core Differentiator: Skill Gap vs. Evidence Gap
Here is where SkillSetu fundamentally innovates.

We strictly separate **Claimed Skills** from **Demonstrated Capabilities**. If a candidate claims Advanced Docker proficiency but has zero verified code artifacts, SkillSetu does not grant unearned credit. Instead, it marks an **Evidence Gap**.

Our engine separates a candidate's *knowledge deficit* from their *evidence deficit*. If they already understand Docker, our **Adaptive Roadmap** skips beginner video lectures and directs them immediately to **BUILD** — creating a containerized microservices project and submitting verifiable repository proof.

---

### [1:50 – 2:30] The Closed Loop: Evidence to Re-Evaluation
And when that candidate submits their project, SkillSetu completes the loop.

The system verifies the artifact, upgrades their capability status to **Demonstrated**, advances the roadmap version, records the exact change rationale, and shifts focus to the Next Best Action — such as *Kubernetes Orchestration*.

The roadmap is never a static list of video links; it is a living development plan that evolves in real-time as the candidate builds verifiable proof.

---

### [2:30 – 3:00] Enterprise Impact & Technical Architecture
For organizations, the same capability model powers multi-horizon scenario planning. Leaders can model whether to **Hire**, **Upskill**, or **Redistribute** talent across 12 to 36 months, with zero invasive employee surveillance or opaque ranking.

Under the hood, SkillSetu runs on a production Node.js Express orchestration backend, a high-performance Python FastAPI service with 384-dimensional multilingual embeddings, and PostgreSQL with pgvector.

---

### [3:00 – 3:15] Closing Takeaway
To summarize:
**SkillSetu does not rely on keyword matching. It connects demonstrated capability, role requirements, market direction, and evidence-based development into one transparent, explainable loop.**

Thank you! We are ready for your questions.
