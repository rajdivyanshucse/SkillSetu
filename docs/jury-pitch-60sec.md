# SkillSetu — 60-Second Elevator Pitch
**Target Spoken Duration**: 50 – 60 Seconds | **Focus**: Problem $\rightarrow$ Solution $\rightarrow$ Differentiator $\rightarrow$ Impact

---

### Verbatim Script:

> "Conventional career platforms rely heavily on resume keywords, creating a massive gap between what talent claims and what they can actually build.
>
> **SkillSetu** is a continuous capability development platform that replaces keyword matching with evidence-backed intelligence.
>
> Grounded in official **O\*NET** and **ESCO** taxonomies and multilingual semantic embeddings, SkillSetu evaluates what candidates can actually demonstrate, separates their **Skill Gaps** from their **Evidence Gaps**, and generates a dynamic 5-stage **Adaptive Roadmap**: *Learn, Practice, Build, Validate, and Re-evaluate*.
>
> When a candidate builds a verified project, the system re-evaluates their capability state, advances their roadmap, and dynamically shifts to the Next Best Action. For enterprises, it powers transparent workforce scenario modeling to Hire, Upskill, or Redistribute talent.
>
> **SkillSetu closes the loop from capability discovery to verified career growth.**"
